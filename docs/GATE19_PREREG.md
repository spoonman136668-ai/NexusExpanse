# Gate 19 preregistration

Replay no old trial. Generate three new hidden-lock DEV worlds from the exact Gate 16 seed-42, 43 and 44 observation graphs and disjoint start/goal assignments. A fourth independently allocated raw action flips a hidden lock and leaves the visible start byte unchanged. The other actions are visible no-ops while locked and follow the learned graph when open. A goal relocks the world. Every attempt starts locked and requires at least three actions. The second phase silently rotates all four actions, including the lock action. Phase 3 returns to the original mapping in a fresh process.

Give all arms the same 24 goals per phase, a maximum of six actions per goal, identical shuffled candidate orders and only the actual successor after acting. Early success ends the attempt and actual steps are recorded. The learnable posterior gets an attempt boundary with no argument. It never receives the bit, the identity of the switch action, an oracle intermediate, or a world label.

The PO extension tracks a two-mode movement posterior and bounded per-bank Beta counts over raw actions. Start the probability at .05. When likely stalled, probe the highest toggle estimate with a .35/(count+1) exploration bonus. Follow a visible no-op with a distinct action chosen by the existing planner where possible. Movement after the second action retroactively credits the previous probe. A second no-op gives negative credit when its action differs. No-op updates marginalize the two-mode toggle transition using the current Beta mean. Movement sets the estimate to .98. A completed goal resets transient belief. The Markov model updates on realized movement for PO arms. The unchanged Gate 16 Markov control observes all realized outcomes. Posterior values are internal estimates, and ambiguous sequences can credit the wrong action.

Run random, one-step reactive, persistent unmodified Gate 16 policy, fresh PO planner and persistent PO planner. Only fresh PO memory resets by experimental intervention at phase boundaries. The persistent PO and Markov controls must restore exact bytes and prediction digests. The budget is 40,000 serialized adaptive bytes inclusive of the two-mode state, measured after every transition. Fixed codebooks and transient forecast tensors are outside the serialized budget.

Freeze before scoring. Run one phase per invocation with `--seed N --arm NAME --directory OUTSIDE --epoch-limit 1`. Verify contiguous immutable intents, snapshots and phase receipts before continuation or `--merge OUTSIDE/result.json`. Preserve the trace and actual consequences for all 45 cells.

PASS requires strictly winning both total and cold completions versus all four controls in 3/3 seeds, plus exact restore and the budget. With engineering qualified, MIXED means a complete win in two seeds while beating Markov totals in at least two, or winning Markov totals in all three while tying or trailing fresh PO in cold return in at least one. Other completed misses fail. This fixture bounds the claim to synthetic hidden-lock DEV. Publish raw evidence to `evidence/gate19-po-agency-dev-001.json`.
