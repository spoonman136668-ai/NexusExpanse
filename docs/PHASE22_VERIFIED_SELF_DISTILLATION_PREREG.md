## Decision rules

**Engineering.** Require zero shared 32-byte windows, zero donor forwards, SHA-correct streams that load unmodified through the existing raw training entry, and independent verification of 100% of published traces without gaps or extra bytes. The first seed replays the full harvest from the same archive and freshly read tasks. Require exactly matching streams and audit metadata. Require identical initialization, update/target schedules, and stream lengths between active adaptation arms. Snapshot parity, probability round-trip, and the 100 future-suffix probes on the first seed's verified export must have exact `0.0` differences. Require final bare pytest with zero failures, skips, or warnings, legacy prefix causality at `0/100`, and clean Git integrity and diff checks.

**Scientific DEV.** A budget counts as a strict win in a seed only when verified self-distillation beats both the unchanged base and matched greedy adaptation on held macro accuracy. PASS requires both budgets to win jointly in all five seeds. MIXED means at least one budget wins in at least one seed and the joint PASS criterion falls short. FAIL means no strict win at either budget. Failed scored engineering makes science INVALID. Report all three arms even if a control wins. Independent scientific qualification and generalization beyond these task templates remain open.
