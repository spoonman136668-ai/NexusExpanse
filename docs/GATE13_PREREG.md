# Gate 13 preregistration

Qualification is Linux CPU on the reused Gate 10/11 raw-byte DEV corpus. Gate 12 evidence selects causal attention plus DirectMonarch unconditionally. DeltaNetMonarch enters only if Gate 12 passed its engineering check and reached at least 50% held-out math and 75% held-out causal in each seed. Verify the committed Gate 12 receipt hash and freeze the selected suite before training. The backbone is the architecture from the supervised comparison. All arms start from the same seeded initialization per backbone. Starting from trained supervised weights would invalidate the untrained floor and the comparison of objectives.

## Data, matched arms, and fitting

Use the exact Gate 10 360-row training and 120-row eval manifest. Expand only math and science to 1,200 each using the frozen Gate 11 generator. Keep all 120 original raw-code train spans and all original eval rows. Each epoch independently permutes each domain by seed plus epoch. Each of 60 batches contains 20 math, 2 code, and 20 causal rows. Every expanded row is seen once per epoch by every trained arm. Code has no synthetic expansion. No repeats within an epoch and no evaluation-data-dependent decisions.

Run seeds 42, 43, 44, dim 64, two layers, four heads, always-on DirectMonarch. The four arms are untrained, next-byte CE, pure InfoNCE-JEPA with per-domain VICReg, and the sum of CE and the same JEPA objective. Train each nonrandom arm for 10 epochs, 600 optimizer steps, single-threaded deterministic PyTorch, AdamW .003/.01 and gradient norm cap 1.0. CE scores actual adjacent bytes on context plus future and masks padding. JEPA predicts the matched future's last-valid EMA latent from only the context's last-valid latent. Use a 64–128–64 DirectMonarch predictor, cosine InfoNCE temperature .1 with other in-batch futures as negatives, and cosine EMA .90 to .99. Apply the existing per-domain VICReg to context and predictor output, with variance weight 25 and off-diagonal covariance weight 1 for each. Equal data and updates do not imply equal FLOPs or predictive targets.

## Evaluation and decision

Fit every primary retrieval readout and probe on the same original 360-row train-only manifest, including the control. This retains the original Gate 10 task and the exact histogram comparator. Evaluate on the original 120 rows. Rank the true continuation among the same stable in-domain 16-candidate sets using cosine similarity, and report Top-1 and MRR, including ranks. Recompute and require the historical 20% histogram Top-1. The two frozen latent probes are ridge readouts for 4-class math and 2-class causal with outputs and labels preserved. Direct latent retrieval and native EMA retrieval are diagnostics. Report per-domain effective rank and mean standard deviation and time for training, inference, and fresh-process restore. Verify cold digest and that evaluation leaves weights unchanged.

Each candidate arm and backbone must independently reach effective rank at least 8.0 and mean standard deviation at least .50 in all nine seed-domain cells, strictly beat the matched untrained arm on Retrieval Top-1 and both probes in each seed, and strictly beat 20% Retrieval Top-1 in each seed. PASS additionally requires at least +5.0 percentage points over matched next-byte CE in every seed on Retrieval Top-1 or on both relational probes. MIXED means all required lower bars hold and the CE margin misses. FAIL means any lower bar misses. Missing or invalid receipts are unverified. Report per-arm and per-backbone status and the strongest status across the predetermined candidates. These are reused DEV outcomes.

## Execution

Freeze code, unit tests and protocol before execution. With the frozen tree run `python benchmarks/gate13_jepa_proven_backbone_dev.py --directory OUTSIDE --seed N --arm NAME --backbone BACKBONE --epoch-limit 4` in bounded slices. Keep the immutable checkpoint intent, source identity, optimizer and random state, parent hashes and cold receipts. Reconstruct only from the newest contiguous validated epoch. After all required receipts, use `--merge OUTSIDE/result.json` at the frozen commit and commit that raw output as `evidence/gate13-jepa-proven-backbone-dev-001.json`.
