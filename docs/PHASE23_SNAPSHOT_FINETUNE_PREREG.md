**Scientific DEV.** PASS requires strict gains in held macro accuracy over each corresponding baseline at both `K=1` and `K=16` in all five seeds, and increased `K=16` solve totals in at least two of the four non-math categories across the five seeds. MIXED requires at least one strict macro win or one improved non-math category at `K=16` when the full PASS gate is missed. With none, report FAIL. An invalid engineering run is scientifically INVALID. Keep zeros and regressions visible. The comparison is against the initial snapshot. It does not measure official LiveBench performance or isolate curriculum content from further training on other data.
