## Scientific DEV comparison

Hypothesis. Including deterministic worked steps in curriculum continuations improves exact final-answer accuracy over a direct-answer curriculum on unseen multi-step math and logic tasks.

Use seeds 3801 through 3805 on CPU. Within each seed, both arms start from identical seeded FP32 weights, with the same model configuration, optimizer, and training and candidate budgets. The direct arm sees the final answers. The scratchpad arm receives the same prompts and answers with fixed derivations from the same inputs. Each seed generates 96 unique fit pairs and 12 calibration pairs, each pair containing one task per category. Training crops the final buffer to an exact target-block boundary. Training updates may wrap around the fit blocks. This matches optimizer updates and target bytes, so the longer scratchpad records receive fewer repetitions for the same compute budget.

Fit, calibration, and held generators use separate RNG streams and slot or operand ranges. The 12 held tasks per seed consist of six equations of the form `(a*b)+c=` and six three-variable ordering puzzles with two constraints. The task prompt has no worked derivation. Verify generated references and require zero shared 32-byte windows on both direct and scratchpad reference forms before training. The benchmark performs one two-epoch training run per arm and seed, 48 steps per epoch with AdamW, cosine LR starting at 0.001, context 96, one block per micro-batch, and two micro-batches per update. Reproduce the direct run at seed 3801 with separate invocations for the engineering resume comparison.

Score both INT8 snapshots on the same held tasks with `--allow-think --no-episodic --always-wake` behavior. Use the greedy candidate at K=1 and that same candidate plus 15 nucleus samples at K=16, with temperature 0.7, top-p 0.9, and identical proposal seeds for both arms. Bound continuations at 112 bytes for math and 128 for reasoning. A correct deterministic final answer is worth one pass per task. Record both budgets by seed and category, the selected candidate, verifier receipt, decode cost, and snapshot digest.

PASS requires a strict scratchpad win on all five seeds at both budgets, and a positive aggregate scratchpad delta in both categories at both budgets. MIXED requires at least one strict seed win or one positive category delta without meeting PASS. FAIL records the absence of any such improvement.
